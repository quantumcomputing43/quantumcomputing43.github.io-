QUANTUM33 / COMPUTATIONAL RESEARCH

miRNA-21
INFORMATION & SENSING.

Two distinct research questions are kept separate: whether compressed molecular representations retain predefined biological-state information, and whether a proposed sensing model can identify its parameters. Results from computation are not presented as biological or clinical validation.

Evidence status: computational audit in progress. No claim of a validated diagnostic, clinical utility, or demonstrated quantum advantage is made here.

01 / REPRESENTATION & INFORMATION RETENTION

WHAT INFORMATION SURVIVES COMPRESSION?

The core question is whether a smaller molecular representation retains enough information to distinguish predefined biological or functional states without leaking labels or encoding the answer into the representation.

Representations under comparison

  • R0_full: full RNA benchmark reference.
  • PCA25: unsupervised 25-component compression; previous project notes report strong PAM50 discrimination but loss of relevant miR-21 signal.
  • R3_miR21_only: miR-21-only representation.
  • R8D_frozen: frozen 8-dimensional multi-task PLS candidate; promising computationally, not established as biologically minimal.
  • R8D_null: shuffled/permuted control intended to break biological alignment.

Audit contract

Compare the frozen representations with predefined endpoints, nulls, and controls. Do not refit the candidate on validation/test data, select seeds after seeing results, or add variables post hoc.

B → M → X → R(X) → Y

Biological state → molecular processing/context → measured RNA → representation → predefined endpoint.

KNOWN LIMITATION / INPUT BLOCKER

External endpoint validation is not cleared

The correct 2010–2015 PAM50/mortality endpoint file is still a documented blocker in the project record. Until provenance, follow-up, event/censoring definitions, sample IDs, and compatibility with the expression data are verified, survival validation must remain closed.

Interpretation rule: prediction is not mechanism; correlation is not causation; simulation is not biological validation.

02 / SEPARATE WORKSTREAM — SENSING MODEL

BINDING IS NOT IDENTIFIABILITY.

f(C, Kd) = C / (C + Kd)
δω = g · f
Γφ = Γ0 + g²τcf(1−f)

The binding expression is a baseline model. Parameters such as coupling, correlation time, source spectrum, and readout model require independent experimental justification.

Known structural issue: concentration C and dissociation constant Kd are confounded through the ratio C/Kd in this occupancy expression unless independent information or a distinct measurement breaks the symmetry.

PREVIOUS MODEL AUDIT — NOT A NEW EXPERIMENT

The existing project page reported Fisher-information rank 3/5 and condition number approximately 2.302 × 1017. These values indicate severe ill-conditioning in that reported model configuration; the underlying raw artifacts must be recovered and checked before treating the exact figures as independently reproducible.

Increasing Monte Carlo sample count alone cannot repair structural non-identifiability.

03 / EVIDENCE, LIMITS & REPRODUCIBILITY

NO CLAIM WITHOUT A TRACEABLE ARTIFACT.

EVIDENCE RULE

Separate claims

Computational performance, mechanistic interpretation, biological validation, and clinical utility are four different levels of evidence.

REPRODUCIBILITY

Freeze the protocol

Record dataset provenance, sample IDs, code/version, seed, representation transform, metric, controls, threshold, and stopping rules before execution.

NULL & CONTROLS

Try to falsify

Use shuffled-label or representation controls, nuisance-only alternatives, perturbation tests, and independent seeds. A valid result may be inconclusive or a failure.
