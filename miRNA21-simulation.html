From hairpin to guide strand

MicroRNA genes are transcribed into longer primary transcripts. Processing creates a hairpin precursor; Dicer helps generate a short duplex, and one guide strand can be loaded into an Argonaute-containing silencing complex.

miR-21 is a small non-coding RNA. This graphic simplifies several processing steps into one visual.
