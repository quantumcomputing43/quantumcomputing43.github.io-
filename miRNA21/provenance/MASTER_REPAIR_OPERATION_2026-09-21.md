## Mission

Perform one integrated repository and scientific-method repair pass. Search, reconcile, repair, test, and document all recoverable issues without inventing scientific information.

## Global invariants

1. Experiment/method validity precedes interpretation.
2. Library and repository evidence are searched before external redesign.
3. Historical V3/V4/V5 records never become V1 specifications.
4. Missing R8D provenance or V5 executable source is a BLOCK, not an invitation to reconstruct.
5. No post-hoc threshold, metric, endpoint, feature, effect size, or mechanism changes.
6. Synthetic simulation is not biological validation.
7. Association is not causation.
8. Structural non-identifiability is not repaired by more samples.
9. Infrastructure success is never reported as scientific success.
10. NUP153 remains methodological context only; do not insert it into R8D.
